Questions, answered

Do I have to add a photo?+

No. Adding a photo is completely optional. If you do add one, it stays blurred and hidden until both you and your match agree to reveal.

Is this only for CPUT students?+

Yes — this is built specifically for CPUT, across all campuses: Bellville, District Six, Cape Town, Mowbray, Wellington, and Granger Bay.

How does matching actually work?+

We look at your interests, your preferences, and your prompt answers to suggest one person at a time — not a wall of profiles to scroll through.

What happens after we reveal?+

You keep chatting, now knowing who's on the other end. Nothing forces you to meet up — the reveal just removes the anonymity from the conversation.
